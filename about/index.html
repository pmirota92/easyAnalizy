About

🌟 Meet the Power BI Developer & Automation Specialist who brews more than just coffee!

When I’m not optimizing processes in the world of automation, you can find me diving into the pages of captivating nonfiction—preferably with a steaming cup of coffee in hand, complete with a latte art masterpiece. ☕✨

📚 Shaped by Books: My curious mind has been sparked by some unforgettable reads:

-🌌 “Packing For Mars. The Curious of Life in the Void” by Mary Roach, reminding me that today’s “obvious” facts were once groundbreaking discoveries which required the work of many brilliant minds to achieve!

-🔍 “Everybody Lies: Big Data, New Data, and What the Internet Can Tell Us About Who We Really Are”, opened my eyes to the fact that data is everywhere—and we’re constantly leaving traces, even when innocently using search engines for everyday questions. Investing in data analysis is the future, as those who can swiftly and effectively extract insights will hold a real advantage.

-📊 Finally, Tim Harford’s “How to Make the World Add Up” emphasizes the importance of skepticism. We must question results and verify methodologies instead of accepting everything at face value—because truth is often more nuanced than we think!

Furthermore, I’m also…

✈️ Curious Explorer: As a budding traveler, I’m eager to step out of my comfort zone and embrace the thrill of discovering new cultures and flavors, one adventure at a time. 🍽️🌍

📊 Data Enthusiast: And because life is all about balance, I’m constantly expanding my horizons in data analysis and machine learning.

Why was this blog created?

Initially, I thought of it as a space for learning and sharing knowledge. But now, it’s become a place where I want to showcase diverse approaches to working with data—from best practices in Power BI and powerful solutions in Power Query to data analysis techniques with Python and machine learning. Here, you’ll find not only tips on automation but also inspiration, new features, best practices, insights, and all things data.

If I manage to spark someone’s interest or make their work with data easier, I’ll consider the goal achieved!
